What we do

Statistical services built for complexity.

From exploratory data analysis to advanced predictive modelling, our work is grounded in mathematical rigour and delivered with practical clarity for decision-makers at every level.

  1. Statistical Analysis

    Descriptive and inferential statistics, hypothesis testing, ANOVA, regression, and multivariate analysis tailored to your research questions.

  2. Predictive Modelling

    Machine learning, forecasting, time-series analysis, and risk modelling to anticipate outcomes and optimise decision-making.

  3. Survey Design & Analysis

    Expert questionnaire design, sampling strategy, weighting, and full statistical analysis of survey data at any scale.

  4. Research Consulting

    End-to-end support for academic and corporate research, from study design and power analysis through to peer-review-ready reporting.

  5. Data Visualisation

    Interactive dashboards and publication-quality charts that communicate statistical findings to technical and non-technical audiences alike.

  6. Expert Witnessing

    Credible, court-ready statistical evidence and expert testimony for litigation, regulatory proceedings, and dispute resolution.

How we work

Rigour at every stage of the analysis.

Great statistical work demands as much rigour in communication as in computation.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery & Scoping

    We invest time understanding your objectives, data landscape, and the decisions the analysis must inform.

  2. Step 02

    Data Preparation

    Cleaning, validation, and transformation with full audit trails, because the quality of analysis depends on the quality of data.

  3. Step 03

    Analysis & Modelling

    Selecting and applying the most appropriate statistical methods, with transparent assumptions and sensitivity testing throughout.

  4. Step 04

    Reporting & Presentation

    Clear, structured reports and presentations that translate statistical findings into actionable intelligence for your team.
